# Testing math
A family of Hermitian connections

$$
g(\nabla^t_XY,Z)=g(\nabla^{LC}_XY,Z)+\frac{1-t}{4}c(X,Y,Z)-\frac{t+1}{4}c(X,JY,JZ) ,
$$

where $c=Jd\omega$ with $\omega=g(J\cdot,\cdot)$.
